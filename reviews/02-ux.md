# UX review

Date: 2026-10-12 · Target: https://tonegliseavalenciennes.fr/ (production).

This report was written in two passes on the same day. The first pass (architect) read the page code and six screenshots. The second pass (UX designer) is a full inventory: every public page was opened in a browser at computer and phone size, and the menus, lists, video player and cookie choices were operated. Findings keep their first-pass numbers; U12 to U20 are new. Where the second pass contradicts the first, the text says so.

## Method and limits

First pass:

- Read the HTML of the 29 published pages, two sermon posts, the sermon category, an author archive and a 404 page, fetched once each.
- Took screenshots of the home page (1440 px and 390 px wide) and of five inner pages, with cookies refused.

Second pass:

- Opened 35 addresses in a headless Chromium at 1440×900 and at 390×844 (phone, touch): the 27 pages of the sitemap, two sermon posts, the sermon category, the author archive, a search, a 404, page 2 of the sermon list and one leftover theme page. Each was scrolled to the bottom and captured in full.
- Five key pages were also opened at 320, 768 and 1024 px.
- Operated: the phone menu and its sub-menu, the computer drop-down, keyboard Tab through the header, the home carousel, the sermon list pagination, the donation accordion, the cookie banner (refuse, preferences, accept) and the video player in both cookie states.
- Measured in the browser: text sizes, colour contrast, size of tappable areas, line length, page weight, layout shift.
- 23 new screenshots are in `reviews/screenshots/`, prefixed `p2-`.

Limits of both passes:

- No real visitor was observed and no analytics were available, so priorities are judgement, not measurement.
- **No form was submitted**, so confirmation and error messages were not seen.
- Weights and timings come from one fast connection with no throttling; they show what is heavy, not what a visitor on 4G waits.
- The home page changed between the two passes (see "Changes seen since the first pass").

## Changes seen since the first pass

Seen on the live site during the second pass:

- The home page no longer has the "Je suis nouveau" block or the "Les évènements" block.
- `/decouvrir-la-foi/` and `/la-technique/` now answer 404.
- The author archive is now `/author/fdesmet/`, titled with the administrator's full name (see U10).

`reviews/screenshots/home-desktop-events.jpg` shows the home page as it was before these changes.

## Who comes, and for what

Three visitors matter most for a local church site:

1. **The newcomer** — "What is this church, when and where do I go, will I fit in?"
2. **The member** — "Where is last Sunday's sermon? What is on this month?"
3. **The person in need** — "Can someone pray for me or meet me? Can I get food aid?"

The site serves the member's first question well and the other needs poorly.

### The three paths, walked on a phone

**Newcomer, Saturday evening, phone in hand.** The first screen is a photograph and three rotating words. The menu button is there but cannot be seen (U12). They scroll: a welcome sentence, four buttons that all assume they already belong (contact, interview, prayer, volunteering), sermon thumbnails. The address and "Dimanche - 10h00" arrive on the fourth screen, in white 13 px text on light blue. Nothing can be tapped to call or to open a map. Nothing says what a Sunday is like. If they open the latest sermon to get a feel, its text says "Dimanche à 09h30".

**Member, Monday, looking for yesterday's sermon.** This works: the sermon is on the home page, one tap away. With cookies refused the video is replaced by a button asking to accept marketing cookies; with cookies accepted it plays. Looking for a sermon from last spring is another story: the list gives day and month without the year, has no filter, and stops after 42 sermons (U5).

**Person in need, any day.** "Demande de prière" is the third button on the home page, which is good. The form is short. Nothing says who will read the request, whether it stays confidential, or when someone answers. For food aid, "Entraide" is three levels deep (L'église → Les départements → Entraide) and the page describes the association at length ("tous les mercredis" is mentioned in passing) without saying at what time, where, or how to ask for help.

### U1 — Newcomer path

First pass: the home page had a block "Vous cherchez une église où grandir et vivre votre foi ?" with a button "Je suis nouveau" leading to a 404, and `/decouvrir-la-foi/` was filler text.

Second pass: the block and the filler page are gone. The dead end is closed, and nothing replaced it. `/je-suis-nouveau/` still answers 404 for anyone who kept the link.

There is still no "what to expect on a Sunday" content anywhere: length of the service, what happens with children, parking, how to get there, whether one can just walk in. The closest thing is the pastoral welcome on "Le couple pastoral", which is warm but speaks about faith, not about the visit.

Proposal: one "Première visite" page answering those questions, linked from the first screen and the menu.

### U2 — Contradictory practical information

- Sunday service: "Dimanche - 10h00" on the home and contact pages; "Dimanche à 09h30" in every 2026 sermon post; "tous les dimanches de 10h à 12h" on Génération Kids.
- Postcode: "59000 Valenciennes" in the footer, home page, contact page, donation page (the address for cheques) and cookie policy; "59300 Valenciennes" in sermon posts. 59300 is the Valenciennes postcode; 59000 is Lille. This is the address people will type into a GPS and write on a cheque envelope.
- Street: "rue du chauffour" on pages, "Rue du Chaufour" in sermon posts.
- E-mail: `secretariat@add-valenciennes.fr` shown on pages, a `mailto:` link to `contact@eglisevalenciennes.fr` in the contact page code, and a Gmail address in the cookie policy.

Confirmed unchanged in the second pass. A visitor who notices any of these stops trusting all of them.

Proposal: one source of truth for address, times, phone and e-mail, reused everywhere.

### U3 — Empty events

First pass: the home page had a heading "Les évènements" followed by blank space.

Second pass: that block is gone from the home page. The events page itself is still a title and 190 px of white, and "Évènements" is still one of six top-level menu entries and a footer link. To a newcomer this reads as "nothing happens here".

Proposal: either feed the events (even recurring ones: Sunday service, Tuesday prayer, youth on Saturday) or remove the menu entry until someone owns it.

### U4 — First screen

The first screen is a full-height photograph (900 px of 900 on a computer, 844 of 844 on a phone) with an animated headline cycling "Une église pour moi / nous / toi". No address, no service time, no button. On a phone the practical block "Où sommes-nous ?" starts about 2,700 px down, the fourth screen. The four buttons under the welcome text (contact, interview request, prayer request, volunteering) all serve people who already know the church.

On a first visit the cookie banner covers the bottom third of that screen on a phone and dims the whole page on a computer (`p2-first-visit-mobile.jpg`, `p2-first-visit-desktop.jpg`), so the very first thing the site asks a stranger is a question about cookies.

Proposal: first screen = who we are in one sentence, next service day and time, address, one button "Première visite".

### U12 — Invisible menu button on the phone home page

`p2-home-mobile-first-screen.jpg`, `p2-home-mobile-menu-button-zoom.jpg`.

On the home page at phone width the menu button is a white three-bar icon, 21×34 px, placed over the projector screen in the photograph, which is almost white. Measured on the capture: icon white, background between `#bbc6ce` and white, a contrast of 1.7:1 at best and none at worst. In practice the top right corner looks empty. On every inner page the same icon is yellow on a dark blue banner and is easy to see.

Consequences on a phone, where the home page is the usual entry point:

- The only visible ways out of the home page are the four buttons, the sermon cards and the footer links, 3,500 px down.
- "L'église", "Faire un don" and the departments look unreachable.

When found, the menu itself works well (`p2-mobile-menu-open.jpg`): full-screen, 18 px labels, rows 47 px high, "L'église" opens its five sub-pages in place. Two reservations: the arrow that opens the sub-menu is 27×27 px, and the menu lists only Facebook and YouTube while the footer has Instagram too.

Proposal: a menu button with its own background, the same on every page, at least 44×44 px, with the word "Menu".

### U13 — Small text and small tap areas on phones

Measured at 390 px wide over the 35 pages:

- Body text is 13 px (38,500 characters), secondary text 12 px, some 11 px, and the nested list on "Entraide" is 9 px (`p2-entraide-mobile-scroll.jpg`). The same text is 16 px on a computer: the theme shrinks text on the device where it is hardest to read. Pinch-zoom is also blocked (accessibility review, A1).
- 21 to 51 tappable items per page are smaller than 44 px in one direction, and almost all of those are under 24 px. The 13 footer links are 15 px high and stacked 24 px apart; the logo link is 18 px high; pagination numbers and date badges are small too.
- "Découvrir les départements" on the home page is 31 px high, where the other buttons are 47 px.
- The donation page's only action for a bank transfer is the three-letter link "ici".

The form fields are the good exception: 65 px high with 17 px text, large enough for a thumb and for iOS not to zoom the page.

Proposal: 16 px minimum body text on phones, 44 px minimum for anything tappable, links named after what they do ("Télécharger le RIB").

### U5 — Sermons

- 303 sermon posts since 2020, about one a week: the most alive part of the site.
- `/les-predications/` shows six per page over seven pages, so **42 sermons, back to November 2025**. The other 261 are not reachable from this page. The category archive lists all of them, ten per page over 31 pages, but nothing links to it from the menu.
- Neither list has a filter by speaker, series, year or Bible book. A search box exists only in the sidebar of sermon pages, which sits below the whole article on a phone.
- **No year anywhere in the lists.** Cards show a round badge "11 OCT". Search results and the "Vous aimerez également" block mix sermons from 2021 to 2026 with nothing to tell them apart (`p2-search-results-desktop.jpg`).
- Pagination on `/les-predications/` replaces the cards without changing the address. Opening `/les-predications/page/2/` shows page 1 again. A member cannot share or bookmark "page 3", and the browser's back button leaves the list instead of returning to the previous page of results.
- Titles mix formats: "Speaker : Title", "Title – Speaker", "Title – Speaker in capitals". Series ("Remplis bien ton ministère", parts 1–3) are not grouped. Titles break mid-word on cards ("minis-tère", "Timo-thée").
- A sermon page shows the thumbnail twice, then the video. Correction to the first pass: with cookies refused the video frame is covered by a button, "Cliquez pour accepter les cookies marketing et activer ce contenu", so the visitor is told why. What is missing is a way to watch without accepting: no link to the video on YouTube exists on the page (only links to the channel).
- With cookies accepted the player loads and works (`p2-sermon-desktop-cookies-accepted.jpg`); the duplicate thumbnail above it stays.
- The text under the video is the same boilerplate on every post (service times, address, social networks), so there is no summary, Bible reference or audio alternative.
- "Vous aimerez également" proposes two sermons that look random (May and October of other years). On a phone their titles are set with lines so tight that they collide (`p2-sermon-mobile-scroll.jpg`).
- The share buttons offer Twitter and Facebook only. A member is more likely to send a sermon by WhatsApp or by copying the link.
- Announcement posts ("Rendez-vous ce dimanche 6 septembre !", "Concert … 90 ANS") sit in the sermon category.

Proposal: one sermon library with speaker, series and date as real fields; full dates; real addresses for each page of results; a clear "watch on YouTube" link as fallback when the embed is blocked.

### U6 — Navigation

Top menu: Accueil, L'église (five sub-pages), Évènements, Prédications, Nous contacter, Faire un don.

- "L'église" itself links to `#`. On a computer the sub-menu opens on hover and on keyboard focus, which is fine; a click on the word does nothing.
- The logo links to `#` instead of the home page, on every page.
- Nine departments exist as pages but are reachable only through "Les départements". Two of them ("Femmes", "Mission") say "Page en construction" and still have a "Découvrir" button on the list.
- Pages not linked from any menu: newsletter sign-up, "Télécharger le logo", "predications-liste".
- "Demande de prière" and "Demande d'entretien" are only in the footer and in home page buttons; they are the pages a person in need looks for.
- The header does not follow the scroll and there is no "back to top" control (U18).
- The active menu entry is yellow on a computer and orange on a phone.
- Breadcrumbs on sermon pages read "Accueil / Tous les articles / … / title", and the title is cut with "…" on a phone. On simple pages the breadcrumb only repeats the title under itself ("Faire un don" / "Accueil / Faire un don").
- Every page title is said twice: once in the banner and once as the first heading of the content ("Nous contacter" / "Nous contacter"), which pushes the content down, most of all on phones.

### U7 — Forms

Correction to the first pass: three of the four contact forms (prayer request, interview request, volunteering) have a visible label above each field. Only the contact form relies on placeholder text that disappears on typing. The newsletter form shows text above its fields but it is not tied to them in the code.

Still true, and seen in the second pass:

- No mark for required fields, no word on who reads the message, whether it is confidential, or when to expect an answer. For a prayer or interview request this reassurance matters most.
- The forms are set not to check anything in the browser, so mistakes can only be reported after sending. Those messages were not seen.
- The contact form is declared in English (`en-US`) while the three others are in French, so its messages may come out in English (not verified).
- No field tells the phone what it holds (name, e-mail, phone), so the phone cannot offer to fill them in.
- The interview form asks for the "Jour de rendez-vous souhaité" as free text, required, with the hint "JJ/MM/AAAA - ex: 15/04/2026" cut to "15/04/20…" on a phone (`p2-interview-form-mobile-scroll.jpg`). The page says appointments are offered "selon vos disponibilités", which a single date cannot express. "C'est à quel sujet" and the message are both required: a person in distress must explain twice before being allowed to send.
- The volunteering form lists 21 areas in one drop-down followed by a free field "Autre".
- The message boxes are 280 px high on a phone: the "Envoyer" button is always below the screen.
- The newsletter form asks for first name, last name and **birthday**, all required, to send a newsletter. The e-mail label is in English ("Email address:"). The examples use a real person's name ("Timothée", "Mele"). On a computer the fields are about 240 px wide in the corner of an empty page (`p2-newsletter-desktop.jpg`).
- Under every form: "En remplissant ce formulaire, vous acceptez notre Politique de confidentialité", in small print, linking to a page that only covers cookies (content review, C3).

### U8 — Getting there, getting in touch

No map and no "itinéraire" link on the home or contact page. Measured over all 35 pages: **no `tel:` link anywhere** on the real site, so the phone number cannot be tapped, and one single `mailto:` link, on the contact page, which goes to a different address from the one displayed (U2). The address is plain text too, so a phone does not offer to open it in a maps app.

The contact page gives the facts in a narrow left column in 13 px on a phone; the form takes the space.

### U9 — Giving

The donation page offers a bank transfer ("Téléchargez le RIB en cliquant ici", a PDF that opens in a new tab) and a cheque by post. No online payment, no QR code, no IBAN to copy as text. On a phone a PDF of bank details is the least convenient form: the IBAN cannot be pasted into a banking app. The cheque address carries the wrong postcode (U2). The tax paragraph is useful and clear, but on a computer it runs 157 characters per line, centred (UI review, V12).

The two options are an accordion; the second is closed on arrival and holds four short lines. Showing both at once would cost less than the click.

### U14 — Movement that cannot be stopped

- The home headline changes word every 1.5 seconds, forever. During each change two words overlap (`p2-home-mobile-scroll.jpg`).
- The sermon carousel on the home page moves by one card every few seconds. A visitor reading the newest title sees it slide away. Its arrows sit half outside the screen on a computer (`p2-home-desktop-carousel.jpg`) and it holds each of the six sermons twice.
- The browser setting "reduce motion" was switched on for a test: both keep moving.

Neither has a pause control (accessibility review, A7).

Proposal: a still headline; the three latest sermons as a plain row.

### U15 — Keyboard

Tabbing through the header on a computer (`p2-desktop-menu-keyboard-focus.jpg`):

- Every link has its focus outline removed. The only sign of focus is the text turning yellow, the same yellow as the active page, on a photograph or on white (1.7:1). On most links a sighted keyboard user cannot tell where they are.
- The sub-menu of "L'église" opens when it receives focus: good.
- After the visible menu entries, Tab enters a second copy of the menu (the phone menu) that is not on screen, before reaching the page.
- No link lets a keyboard user jump to the content (accessibility review, A4).
- Form fields do show focus, with a yellow border, but yellow on the field's beige is 1.7:1.
- The donation accordion titles are not buttons and do not say whether they are open or closed.

### U16 — Cookie choice

- First visit: covered in U4.
- Refusing is as easy as accepting, which is right. The three buttons do not look alike, though: "Tous les cookies" and "Voir les préférences" are white, "Refuser" is outlined (UI review, V3).
- After any choice a tab "Paramètres" stays at the bottom right of every page, on top of the content. (In the full-page captures it appears at mid-height; that is an effect of the capture.)
- Refusing costs the member the video (U5) with no other way to watch. The choice offered is "marketing cookies or no sermon".
- In the preferences panel "Statistiques" and "Marketing" switches are orange when off, which reads as "on" (`p2-cookie-preferences-desktop.jpg`).

Proposal for the redesign: a player that loads only on click (no cookie before the click), which removes the banner's main reason to exist.

### U17 — Search

`/?s=priere` (`p2-search-results-desktop.jpg`):

- The banner reads "Search: priere", in English.
- Pages and sermons are mixed in one grid of cards, 11 pages long, newest first, with day-and-month badges and no year.
- Each sermon's excerpt is the boilerplate ("Culte du … Ton Église À Valenciennes Culte : Dimanche à 10h00 …"), so results cannot be told apart by their text, and the old ones repeat the old service time.
- The page "Demande de prière", the best answer to this search, comes far down the first page, as a card with no image.
- The search box is not in the header; it was only seen in the sidebar of sermon pages.

### U18 — Long pages, no way back, no next step

- Phone pages are long: 5,000 px for "Entraide", 7,100 px for the departments, 10,600 px for the terms. The header does not stay, and there is no "back to top".
- Department pages end on their last paragraph, then the footer. No "contact the leader", no "come on Sunday", no link to the next department or back to the list. Génération Kids answers every question a parent has and then offers nothing to do about it.
- The 404 page (`p2-404-desktop.jpg`) is friendly ("Mince…") and offers one button to the home page: no search, no list of the main pages.

### U10 — Leaks from the page builder

- `/predications-liste/` shows the heading "All Posts" and its browser title is just the site name; the 404 banner says "URL not found" above the French message; search says "Search:"; pagination says "Next".
- The author archive is public at `/author/fdesmet/` and is titled with the administrator's full name: every sermon is signed by the person who maintains the site, and the address gives away the login name (see the security review).
- Theme parts are published as pages and listed in the sitemap. `/layouts/footer-default/` shows the theme's demo footer: "1234W 12th Street, Indianapolis", "(123) 123-4567", "AxiomThemes {{Y}}. All Rights Reserved." and a broken image (`p2-theme-demo-page-desktop.jpg`). The sitemap also lists dozens of page-builder templates.
- The cookie banner's preference panel contains unreplaced placeholders (`{vendor_count}`, `{title}`) in the page code.

### U11 — Speed and weight

Measured in the second pass, one fast connection, empty cache for the first page only:

- Home page: 3.7 MB and 61 requests on a computer, 3.3 MB and 83 requests on a phone. Images are 2.5 MB of that (the hero photograph alone is 1.1 MB on a phone), style sheets 600 KB, scripts 350 KB.
- **"Les départements" on a phone: 6.6 MB.** Three logos account for most of it: "Une prière pour toi" 3.1 MB, Punch Group 1.1 MB, LSV 1.0 MB, each displayed about 250 px wide.
- Other pages reuse what is already loaded and add 160 to 450 KB.
- Largest element shown after 0.4 to 2.9 s on this connection. Layout shift is nil on phones and reaches 0.13 on the long sermon lists on a computer.
- Server response 0.35–0.9 s per page.

No Lighthouse or field measurement was run; do one on a throttled connection before setting targets for the redesign.

### U19 — Tablet width

At 768 px (`p2-home-tablet-768.jpg`) the site keeps the computer layout: the header shows one menu word and a round yellow button, the headline touches the left edge of the screen, and the four action buttons merge into one blue bar with two-line labels. Nothing overflows sideways at 320, 768 or 1024 px.

### U20 — New tabs

Eight to sixteen links per page open a new tab with no warning: the social links, the three links of the footer credit line, the bank details PDF, and the bare addresses written in brackets on "Notre histoire" ("(https://assemblees-de-dieu.org/)").

## What works

- The sermon feed is current to the day (last post 11 October 2026).
- Sermon cards have meaningful thumbnails.
- The department pages that are written (Kids, Punch Group, ConneXion, Entraide, LSV) answer real questions: who, what age, when.
- The phone layout does not scroll sideways at any width tested, and the full-screen phone menu is clear once opened.
- Form fields are large and comfortable on a phone, and three forms have real labels.
- The prayer request is one tap from the home page and asks for only three things.
- The cookie banner offers "Refuser" at the same level as "Tous les cookies", and the blocked video says why it is blocked.
- The 404 page speaks like a person.

## For the redesign, in order

1. A first screen and a "Première visite" page built for the newcomer (U1, U4).
2. One set of practical facts, tappable: call, write, open in maps (U2, U8).
3. A menu that is visible and identical on every page, on every device (U12, U6).
4. Text and tap areas sized for phones first (U13).
5. A sermon library with full dates, filters, shareable addresses and a no-cookie way to watch (U5, U16).
6. Forms that say who reads them and ask for less (U7).
7. Every page ends with a next step (U18).
8. Nothing moves unless the visitor asks (U14).
