# UX review

Date: 2026-10-12 · Target: https://tonegliseavalenciennes.fr/ (production).

## Method and limits

- Read the HTML of the 29 published pages, two sermon posts, the sermon category, an author archive and a 404 page, fetched once each.
- Took screenshots of the home page (1440 px and 390 px wide) and of five inner pages, with cookies refused.
- No real visitor was observed and no analytics were available, so priorities below are judgement, not measurement. No form was submitted, so confirmation and error messages were not seen.

## Who comes, and for what

Three visitors matter most for a local church site:

1. **The newcomer** — "What is this church, when and where do I go, will I fit in?"
2. **The member** — "Where is last Sunday's sermon? What is on this month?"
3. **The person in need** — "Can someone pray for me or meet me? Can I get food aid?"

The site serves the member's first question well and the other needs poorly.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Finding |
|---|----------|---------|
| U1 | High | The newcomer path is broken: the "Je suis nouveau" button leads to a 404 |
| U2 | High | Practical information contradicts itself (time, postcode, e-mail) |
| U3 | High | The events section and page are empty |
| U4 | High | The first screen says nothing practical |
| U5 | Medium | Sermons: 303 videos with no way to browse them, and a confusing player |
| U6 | Medium | Navigation hides half the site and has dead ends |
| U7 | Medium | Forms give little guidance |
| U8 | Medium | No map, no directions, phone and e-mail are not tappable |
| U9 | Medium | Giving is limited to downloading a bank details PDF |
| U10 | Low | Technical pages leak into the visitor's path |
| U11 | Low | Speed and weight |

### U1 — Newcomer path

The home page contains a block "Vous cherchez une église où grandir et vivre votre foi ?" with a button "Je suis nouveau" linking to `/je-suis-nouveau/`, which returns 404. The page that looks written for this purpose, `/decouvrir-la-foi/` ("Et si Dieu existait vraiment ?"), is filler text ("Lorem ipsum") and is not linked from anywhere.

There is no "what to expect on a Sunday" content anywhere: length of the service, what happens with children, parking, how to get there, whether one can just walk in.

Proposal: one "Première visite" page answering those questions, linked from the first screen and the menu.

### U2 — Contradictory practical information

- Sunday service: "Dimanche - 10h00" on the home and contact pages; "Dimanche à 09h30" in every 2026 sermon post.
- Postcode: "59000 Valenciennes" in the footer, home page, contact page, donation page and cookie policy; "59300 Valenciennes" in sermon posts. 59300 is the Valenciennes postcode; 59000 is Lille. This is the address people will type into a GPS and write on a cheque envelope.
- Street: "rue du chauffour" on pages, "Rue du Chaufour" in sermon posts.
- E-mail: `secretariat@add-valenciennes.fr` shown on pages, a `mailto:` link to `contact@eglisevalenciennes.fr` in the contact page code, and a Gmail address in the cookie policy.

A visitor who notices any of these stops trusting all of them.

Proposal: one source of truth for address, times, phone and e-mail, reused everywhere.

### U3 — Empty events

The home page has a heading "Les évènements" followed by blank space and a button "Voir tous les évènements". The events page has a title and nothing else. "Évènements" is one of six top-level menu entries. To a newcomer this reads as "nothing happens here".

Proposal: either feed the events (even recurring ones: Sunday service, Tuesday prayer, youth on Saturday) or remove the section and the menu entry until someone owns it.

### U4 — First screen

The first screen is a full-height photograph with an animated headline cycling "Une église pour moi / nous / toi". No address, no service time, no button. On a phone the visitor scrolls a full screen before reading a single fact. The four buttons that follow (contact, interview request, prayer request, volunteering) all serve people who already know the church.

Proposal: first screen = who we are in one sentence, next service day and time, address, one button "Première visite".

### U5 — Sermons

- 303 sermon posts since 2020, about one a week: the most alive part of the site.
- `/les-predications/` shows six per page with seven pages (42 sermons); the category archive shows ten per page over 31 pages. Two different lists, neither with a filter by speaker, series, year or Bible book. A search box exists only in the blog sidebar.
- Titles mix formats: "Speaker : Title", "Title – Speaker", "Title – Speaker in capitals". Series ("Remplis bien ton ministère", parts 1–3) are not grouped.
- A sermon page shows the thumbnail twice, then a blocked video frame. With cookies refused, the visitor sees an image and nothing explains that accepting marketing cookies is required to play. The text under the video is the same boilerplate on every post (service times, address, social networks), so there is no summary, Bible reference or audio alternative.
- Announcement posts ("Rendez-vous ce dimanche 6 septembre !") sit in the sermon category.

Proposal: one sermon library with speaker, series and date as real fields; a clear "watch on YouTube" link as fallback when the embed is blocked.

### U6 — Navigation

Top menu: Accueil, L'église (five sub-pages), Évènements, Prédications, Nous contacter, Faire un don.

- "L'église" itself links to `#`: a tap on it does nothing on desktop.
- The logo links to `#` instead of the home page.
- Nine departments exist as pages but are reachable only through "Les départements". Two of them ("Femmes", "Mission") say "Page en construction".
- Pages not linked from any menu: newsletter sign-up, "Découvrir la foi", "La technique", "Télécharger le logo", "predications-liste".
- "Demande de prière" and "Demande d'entretien" are only in the footer and in home page buttons; they are the pages a person in need looks for.
- Breadcrumbs on sermon pages read "Accueil / Tous les articles / … / title".

### U7 — Forms

- Fields have placeholder text only, which disappears on typing (see accessibility review).
- No indication of which fields are required, no word on who reads the message or when to expect an answer. For a prayer or interview request this reassurance matters.
- The volunteering form lists 20 areas in one dropdown.
- The interview form asks for a "desired day" as free text.
- Contact Form 7 is set to locale `en_US`, so validation messages may appear in English (not verified, no form was submitted).
- The newsletter form has an English label ("Email address:") and a visible-to-robots field "Laissez ce champ vide si vous êtes humain".

### U8 — Getting there, getting in touch

No map and no "itinéraire" link on the home or contact page (the cookie policy mentions Google Maps but no map is rendered). The phone number is plain text, not a `tel:` link, so it cannot be tapped on a phone. The displayed e-mail is not a link either.

### U9 — Giving

The donation page offers a bank transfer ("download the RIB by clicking here", a PDF) and a cheque by post. No online payment, no QR code, no IBAN to copy as text. The tax paragraph is useful and clear.

### U10 — Leaks from the page builder

- `/predications-liste/` shows the heading "All Posts"; the author archive shows "Author page: admin"; the 404 page says "URL not found"; pagination says "Next".
- Theme parts are published as pages (`/layouts/header-default/`).
- The cookie banner's preference panel contains unreplaced placeholders (`{vendor_count}`, `{title}`) in the page code.

### U11 — Speed and weight

- Server response 0.35–0.9 s per page from this network; acceptable.
- Each page loads 24–29 script files and two Google Fonts requests, one of which asks for every weight of Roboto.
- The media library holds 19 files over 1.5 MB, up to 4.9 MB; whether the large originals are served depends on each page (not measured).
- No Lighthouse or field measurement was run; do one before setting targets for the redesign.

## What works

- The sermon feed is current to the day (last post 11 October 2026).
- Sermon cards have meaningful thumbnails and dates.
- The department pages that are written (Kids, Punch Group, ConneXion, Entraide, LSV) answer real questions: who, what age, when.
- The mobile layout does not scroll sideways and the menu collapses properly.
- The cookie banner offers "Refuser" at the same level as "Tous les cookies".
