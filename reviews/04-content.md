# Content review

Date: 2026-10-12 · Target: https://tonegliseavalenciennes.fr/ (production).

## Method and limits

Read the visible text of the 29 published pages, two sermon posts and the archive pages. Facts about the church (pastor, times, address, numbers) cannot be verified from outside: where two pages disagree, both versions are quoted and the church must say which is right. Sermon videos were not watched.

### C1 — Contradictions and stale facts

- **Sunday time**: "Dimanche - 10h00" (home, contact) vs "Culte : Dimanche à 09h30" (every 2026 sermon post). Génération Kids says "tous les dimanches de 10h à 12h".
- **Postcode**: "59000" (footer, home, contact, donation cheque address, cookie policy) vs "59300" (sermon posts). 59300 is Valenciennes.
- **Street**: "rue du chauffour" vs "Rue du Chaufour".
- **E-mail**: `secretariat@add-valenciennes.fr` displayed; `contact@eglisevalenciennes.fr` behind a link on the contact page; a Gmail address in the cookie policy.
- **Pastor**: "Notre histoire" ends with "Depuis septembre 2019, c'est le Pasteur Timothée MELE qui a la responsabilité pastorale de l'Église." "Le couple pastoral" presents "Pasteur Nicolas & Mélanie TESSIER".
- **Name of the church**: "Église Évangélique de Valenciennes", "Ton église à Valenciennes", "ADD Valenciennes", "L'Église de Valenciennes". The logo says the first, the site title the second.
- **Ainé'rgie**: "en 2019, nous sommes allés à Lewarde"; "L'âge requis est de 52 ans" (minimum? exactly?).
- **Entraide**: "Depuis ces 27 années d'existence" (27 as of which year?); "interrompu pendant la pandémie" still stated; the association is called "culturelle" where "cultuelle" or simply "familiale" may be meant; figures (200 households, 850 people, 35 volunteers, 90–100 parcels) are undated.
- **Footer**: "© 2020".
- **Facebook link** in the header carries a tracking leftover (`?ref=br_rs`), the YouTube link ends in `?view_as=subscriber`.

### C2 — Filler and stubs

- `/decouvrir-la-foi/`: heading "Et si Dieu existait vraiment ?" followed by Lorem ipsum and a button labelled "Lorem ipsum... Appel à l'action."
- `/la-technique/`: Lorem ipsum and a button "Rejoindre l'équipe technique".
- `/femmes/` and `/mission/`: "Page en construction", listed as full departments with a "Découvrir" button.
- `/evenements/`: empty.

All five are in the sitemap and therefore offered to search engines. Proposal: unpublish the first two today (no page links to them); for the other three, write three sentences or remove the entries.

### C3 — Legal pages

- **No legal notice ("mentions légales")**: French law (LCEN, art. 6-III) requires any site to name its publisher (for an association: name, registered office, publication director) and its host (name, address, phone). None of this is on the site. The footer credits an agency but not the host.
- **No privacy policy**: "Protection des données" is a Complianz-generated *cookie* policy. The forms say "vous acceptez notre Politique de confidentialité" and link to it. Nothing explains what happens to a prayer request, an interview request or a newsletter sign-up: who reads it, how long it is kept, how to have it deleted.
- **Cookie policy lists services the site does not appear to use**: PayPal, SoundCloud, WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google reCAPTCHA. None was seen on the pages visited. It also keeps a sentence about "les éléments restent dans votre panier jusqu'à votre paiement".
- **"Conditions générales"** is a generated commercial template in 21 sections ("transactions liées à nos produits et services", "restrictions à l'exportation", "soumission d'idée"). It does not describe a church website and commits the church to clauses nobody chose. Proposal: replace it with a short legal notice.
- **Photographs**: several pages show identifiable members, and Génération Kids shows children at a table (faces not visible in the image seen). Confirm that image-rights consent exists, especially for minors.
- **Bank details**: the RIB PDF is public. That is the intent, but say so knowingly: published bank details are used in direct-debit fraud. An IBAN shown as text with the association's exact name is no worse and is easier to use.

### C4 — Voice and audience

The texts are sincere and warm, and written from the inside:

- "Secteur", "département", "pôle", "LSV", "ADD", "CNEF", "intercéder", "communion fraternelle" are used without explanation.
- "Nos valeurs" is a statement of faith in eleven doctrinal points. It is valuable, but it is the only answer offered to "what is this church like?".
- "Une prière pour toi" explains the team's spiritual basis at length and never says how to ask for prayer; the form is on another page it does not link to.
- No page says what a Sunday looks like, how long it lasts, whether one can come as one is.
- The home page welcome ("Nous vous invitons à découvrir nos activités et nos départements, qui font toute la richesse de cette Église") describes the organisation, not what the visitor gains.

The strongest newcomer text on the site is the pastoral couple's welcome. It deserves to be near the top of the home page, with their photo.

### C5 — "Tu" and "vous"

The brand line is "Une église pour **Toi**", the youth page says "Si **tu** as entre 18 et 35 ans", the home page says "Une église où **tu** trouveras ta place" and, two lines below, "**Vous** cherchez une église où grandir et vivre **votre** foi ?". Forms and the welcome paragraph use "vous". Pick one as the rule (the brand suggests "tu") and state the exceptions (legal pages, perhaps forms).

### C6 — Sermon posts

Each 2026 post is a video plus a fixed block: date, service times, address, Instagram and Facebook names as plain text. Older posts carry a line of hashtags instead. No summary, Bible passage, series name or speaker field. Consequences: nothing for a visitor to read before committing to an hour of video, nothing for the search box or Google to match, and the boilerplate block is where the 09h30/59300 contradictions come from, repeated across dozens of posts.

Title formats vary ("Nicolas TESSIER : La victoire se prépare", "Abandonner pour avancer – Ben Girault", "– Cedric Mercier : les Autels D'Abraham principes de croissance pour connaitre Dieu").

### C7 — Search engines and sharing

- No page has a meta description. No SEO plugin is installed.
- No structured data (a `Church`/`PlaceOfWorship` block with address, times and coordinates would feed Google's local panel).
- Page titles are "Page – Ton église à Valenciennes": fine, though none contains "évangélique" or "culte", the words people search.
- `/predications-liste/` has the bare site name as title and "All Posts" as heading.
- Open Graph tags exist on pages but not on archives; no share image was checked.
- The sitemap offers Lorem ipsum pages, stubs, 64 page-builder templates and 9 theme layout parts.
- Image file names and alt texts on stock photos are photographers' names ("jeniffer-araujo-…-unsplash.jpg"); social icons have alt "Facebook-logo", "Instagram_Glyph_Gradient".

### C8 — Spelling, typography, leftovers

- "Eglise évanglique" (footer and Conditions générales) → "Église évangélique".
- "Membres des Assemblées de Dieu" → "Membre".
- Inconsistent capitals and accents: "Eglise/Église", "A Noël/À Noël", "Sainte Cène/Sainte-Cène", "Générations Kids" (once) vs "Génération Kids".
- "SI vous désirez vous engager" (capital I); "Lorsque qu'une demande"; "un fois par mois"; "leurs sont offerts"; a stray "‘" at the end of the Ainé'rgie page; "-«" before "Les Dauphins".
- Age bands overlap on Génération Kids: 6–8 and 8–10, 8–10 and 10–12.
- Missing non-breaking spaces before ":" ";" "?" "!" in many places, straight apostrophes mixed with curly ones.
- English left in the interface: "All Posts", "Author page: admin", "URL not found", "Next", "Email address:", form label "Contact form".
- Cookie banner code contains unreplaced `{vendor_count}` and `{title}`.
