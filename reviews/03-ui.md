# UI review

Date: 2026-10-12 · Target: https://tonegliseavalenciennes.fr/ (production).

## Method and limits

Screenshots taken with a headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390 px wide, cookies refused, on: home, contact, a sermon, departments, donation, Génération Kids. Colours and sizes below are read from screenshots and page code, not measured with a design tool. Hover, focus and animation states were not captured. Screenshots are in `reviews/screenshots/`.

## Overall impression

A 2019 theme demo dressed with the church's logo and photos. The brand elements are good: the logo is distinctive, the photographs are real and warm. The layout around them is generic, inconsistent in places, and wastes most of the first screen.

### V1 — Hero

`screenshots/home-desktop-hero.jpg`. White uppercase text ("UNE ÉGLISE POUR MOI") is laid over a photograph of a congregation: it crosses dark coats, a white shirt, a pink jumper and blue stage lighting. The thin-weight half of the headline disappears over light areas. The projector screen in the photo carries its own large text ("Dieu fera tout comme il promet"), which competes with the headline and the menu. The rotating word animates continuously; during the transition two words overlap (seen in the phone capture).

The menu is white text directly on the same photo with no backing.

### V2 — Yellow links

The active menu item, and the links in the footer credit line ("Assemblées de Dieu", "CNEF", "Artception"), are a yellow-orange on white. In the footer it is barely legible. The child theme also defines an orange `#f26837` for the mobile menu's active state: a third accent.

### V3 — Blues and buttons

Three blues appear on the home page alone: a dark slate blue for the four action buttons, a brighter sky blue for "Voir tous les évènements" / "Voir toutes les prédications" and the "Où sommes-nous ?" panel, and the logo's own royal blue. There is no visible rule for which blue means what. Buttons are square-cornered rectangles in two blues plus one white variant; the cookie banner brings a fourth style (rounded, another blue).

### V4 — Typography

Headings use Montserrat at its heaviest weight (900) for everything from the page title to "Les évènements". When every heading shouts, none leads. Body text is Lato in a mid grey that looks light for long paragraphs (to be measured, see accessibility review). Roboto and Roboto Slab are also loaded by Elementor but do not seem used.

Sermon titles break mid-word on cards ("Ni-colas TESSIER", "Par-tie 2").

### V5 — Space

`screenshots/home-desktop-events.jpg`. Between "Une église pour Toi" and the sermon cards the page is mostly white: an empty events block, then about 150 px above and below each button. The four action buttons are spread unevenly across the width, with different sizes because each fits its label. On the contact page the left column (address) is short and the right column (form) starts 100 px lower inside a faint card.

### V6 — Inner page banner

Every inner page opens with the same blue-tinted stage photograph and a centred title plus an uppercase breadcrumb. It gives consistency but no sense of place: Génération Kids, Faire un don and Nous contacter look identical for the first 400 px. On a phone the banner takes about 40 % of the first screen.

### V7 — Sermon page

`screenshots/sermon-desktop.jpg`. The featured image (a YouTube thumbnail with black letterbox bars) is followed immediately by the video placeholder showing the same thumbnail. A circular date badge overlaps the first image's corner. The sidebar thumbnails are cropped mid-text ("BANDONNE / UR AVANC").

### V8 — Footer

`screenshots/home-desktop-footer.jpg`.
- The Facebook icon does not render: the word "FACEBOOK" appears in its place (its alt text), above full-colour Instagram and YouTube logos of different sizes.
- The logo block, two link columns and social icons are not aligned on a common grid; the left half of the footer is mostly empty.
- "© 2020 – Eglise évanglique de Valenciennes" (date and typo, see content review).

### V9 — Polish

- Department cards alternate image left/right, but the images are logos of very different styles and sizes on grey squares.
- The accordion on the donation page uses the sky blue at full width with a heavy bar; the open panel holds one short sentence.
- Bible quotation on the home page is set in bold italic at a smaller size than the paragraph above it.
- A cookie-settings tab stays visible at the bottom right of every screenshot.

## Assets worth keeping

- The logo, in its colour and white versions.
- The real photographs of the congregation and building.
- The department identities (Génération Kids, Punch Group) as illustrations, if they are still current.
- Montserrat and Lato are a workable pair if weights are used with restraint.

## Note on the child theme

`nazareth-child/functions.php` says "no CSS overrides exist in the child theme" and enqueues only the parent stylesheet, yet `nazareth-child/style.css` holds 228 lines of overrides. Either the parent theme loads the child stylesheet by itself or those overrides are dead. Production serves merged, minified CSS, so this could not be settled from outside; check on the local copy before relying on that file as a record of the current design.
