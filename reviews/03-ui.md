# UI review

Date: 2026-10-12 · Target: https://tonegliseavalenciennes.fr/ (production).

This report was written in two passes on the same day. The first pass (architect) looked at six pages. The second pass (UX designer) is a full visual inventory of the 35 public addresses at computer and phone size, with colours, type sizes and contrast measured in the browser. Findings keep their first-pass numbers; V10 to V17 are new.

## Method and limits

First pass: screenshots taken with a headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390 px wide, cookies refused, on: home, contact, a sermon, departments, donation, Génération Kids. Colours and sizes read from screenshots and page code.

Second pass:

- Full-page captures of 35 addresses at 1440×900 and 390×844, plus five pages at 320, 768 and 1024 px.
- Every visible piece of text was read from the browser with its font, size, weight, colour and background, which gives the counts and contrast ratios below. Contrast is the WCAG ratio; 4.5:1 is the minimum for normal text, 3:1 for large text. Text placed over a photograph cannot be measured this way and is judged on the capture.
- Hover and keyboard-focus states were triggered on the menu, a button, a footer link and a form field.
- 23 new screenshots are in `reviews/screenshots/`, prefixed `p2-`. The home page changed between the passes: `home-desktop-events.jpg` shows a block that is no longer there.

Not done: print styles, dark mode, high-contrast mode, real devices (everything comes from one desktop browser emulating a phone).

## Overall impression

A 2019 theme demo dressed with the church's logo and photos. The brand elements are good: the logo is distinctive, the photographs are real and warm. The layout around them is generic, inconsistent in places, and wastes most of the first screen.

The second pass adds one thing to that picture: there is no system underneath. 43 different text styles on a computer and 46 on a phone, four blues, two yellows and an orange, eleven button variants, and a different heading style on almost every page. Each page was styled by hand in the page builder, and it shows as soon as two pages are put side by side.

### V1 — Hero and header

`screenshots/home-desktop-hero.jpg`, `p2-home-mobile-first-screen.jpg`.

White uppercase text ("UNE ÉGLISE POUR MOI", 72 px on a computer, 54 px on a phone) is laid over a photograph of a congregation with no overlay at all: it crosses dark coats, a white shirt, a pink jumper and blue stage lighting. The thin half of the headline disappears over light areas. The projector screen in the photo carries its own large text ("Dieu fera tout comme il promet"), which competes with the headline and the menu. The rotating word animates continuously; during the transition two words overlap.

The menu is white 16 px text directly on the same photo with no backing; three of the six entries sit on the projector screen, the lightest part of the image.

On a phone:

- The menu icon is white on that same projector screen and cannot be seen (measured 1.7:1 at best; `p2-home-mobile-menu-button-zoom.jpg`). On inner pages the icon is yellow on dark blue: same control, two colours.
- The logo is the colour version with dark lettering, placed on the photograph; on inner pages it is the white version. The logo files are 1,000 to 1,200 px wide and displayed at 144 to 217 px.
- The headline breaks into three lines, "UNE ÉGLISE / POUR / MOI", with the thin first line running to the very edge of the screen.

### V2 — Yellow

Measured:

| Use | Colours | Ratio |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Footer credit links ("Assemblées de Dieu", "CNEF", "Artception"), every page | `#ffbd11` on white | 1.7:1 |
| Active menu entry, computer | `#ffaf00` on photo or white | 1.8:1 on white |
| Date badges and current page number: white text on yellow disc | white on `#ffaf00` | 1.8:1 |
| Footer and menu links on hover | `#ffbd11` on white | 1.7:1 |
| Focus border of form fields | `#ffaf00` on `#f7f3ee` | 1.7:1 |
| Active menu entry, phone | `#f26837` (orange) on white | 3.1:1 |

Yellow is the site's only accent and it fails everywhere it carries text. The orange on phones is a third accent that exists nowhere else.

Proposal: keep a warm accent for surfaces and small marks; never use it for text or behind white text.

### V10 — Phone type sizes

Measured at 390 px over 35 pages:

| Size | Where | Share |
|------|-------|-------|
| 13 px | Body text of every page | About 38,500 characters, the bulk of the site |
| 14 px | Legal pages, buttons | 11,000 |
| 12 px | Footer links, consent line | 7,200 |
| 11 px | Dates, badges | 900 |
| 9 px | Nested list on "Entraide" | 260 |

On a computer the same body text is 16 px. Headings shrink too, but less (page titles 28 px, section titles 20 to 24 px), so on a phone a title can be twice the body size while the body is at the limit of comfort. Pinch-zoom is blocked (accessibility review, A1), so the visitor cannot compensate.

Body text colour is `#5b6b6f` on white: 5.6:1, which passes. (The first pass left this "to measure".) It is grey rather than dark, and at 13 px it reads as faint; headings are `#062e36` at 14.5:1.

### V11 — White on light blue

The sky blue `#3d98d1` carries white text at 3.2:1:

- the whole "Où sommes-nous ?" panel on the home page: address, times, e-mail, phone in 13 to 16 px;
- the button "Voir toutes les prédications";
- the accordion titles on the donation page.

The practical facts of the home page are therefore set in the least legible combination of the page after yellow. The cookie tab "Paramètres" (white on `#4d89bf`) is at 3.7:1. White on the darker button blue `#3a64aa` is 5.9:1 and passes.

### V3 — Blues and buttons

Four blues with text or as fills: `#3a64aa` (main buttons, links), `#3d98d1` (sky blue: one button, the address panel, accordions, the quote band on "Notre histoire"), `#4d89bf` (cookie banner and tab), and the logo's own blue; plus the blue tint laid over the inner-page banner. There is no visible rule for which blue means what.

Eleven button variants were counted on a computer:

- fills: dark blue, sky blue, white, cookie blue;
- corners: square everywhere, 12 px rounded on the cookie controls;
- heights: anywhere from 44 to 51 px for ordinary buttons, 66 to 78 px for two oversized ones, and 31 px for "Découvrir les départements" on a phone;
- text: 14 px bold in most, 16 px bold in two, 12 px regular uppercase for "S'inscrire", 14 px regular for the video notice;
- "En savoir plus" is a text link styled as a button with no fill.

Hover lightens the dark blue slightly (`#3a64aa` to `#476db8`), a change too small to notice.

The cookie banner shows three buttons in three treatments, and its preference switches are orange when off (`p2-cookie-preferences-desktop.jpg`).

### V4 — Typography

Counted in the browser: 43 distinct combinations of font, size, weight and case on a computer, 46 on a phone. A consistent site of this size needs eight to ten.

- Headings use Montserrat at weights 500, 700, 800 and 900. The largest sizes are all at 800 or 900: when every heading shouts, none leads.
- Page-level headings, same level, different pages: 48 px weight 800 ("Soutenir l'église", "Comment faire un don ?", both on one page with nothing to rank them), 32 px bold with an uppercase first word ("BIENVENUE à l'Église…"), 24 px medium typed in capitals ("QU'EST-CE QUE CŒUR SUR LA MAIN ?"), about 16 px bold underlined ("Les horaires de Générations Kids :"), 30 px Lato on legal pages.
- Body text is Lato 16 px on a computer, with 14, 13, 12 and 11 px variants. Roboto and Roboto Slab are also loaded by the page builder and are not used by any visible text.
- Uppercase is used for the hero, breadcrumbs, footer column titles, some section headings and one button.
- The Bible quotation on the home page is bold italic and smaller than the paragraph above it; its source is given as "La Bible." with no reference.
- Sermon titles break mid-word on cards and in lists ("Ni-colas TESSIER", "minis-tère", "Timo-thée", "héri-tage"): automatic hyphenation is on for headings.
- On a phone the "Vous aimerez également" titles have a line height smaller than the type, so lines touch (`p2-sermon-mobile-scroll.jpg`).
- The 404 page sets "404" at 272 px on a computer.

### V5 — Space

`p2-home-desktop-carousel.jpg`, `screenshots/home-desktop-events.jpg` (older state).

- Home, computer: wide empty bands above and below the row of four buttons; the buttons are spread across the page with unequal gaps because each fits its label.
- Events page: a banner, a strip of white, the footer.
- Contact page: the left column (address) is short and the right column (form) starts lower, inside a faint card with a shadow so soft it reads as a rendering fault.
- Page gutters differ on a phone: 55 px on most pages, 85 px on Génération Kids (V12), on a 390 px screen.
- Section spacing is set block by block, with no rhythm from one page to the next: the first content heading sits close under the banner on "Le couple pastoral" and far below it on "Faire un don".

### V6 — Inner page banner

Every inner page opens with the same blue-tinted stage photograph and a centred title plus an uppercase breadcrumb. It gives consistency but no sense of place: Génération Kids, Faire un don and Nous contacter look identical for the first 400 px. On a phone the banner is 330 px high, 40 % of the first screen, and up to 430 px when the title wraps (sermon pages).

The title is white bold on the tinted photo and reads well; the breadcrumb under it is thinner and lighter. On a sermon the breadcrumb wraps over three lines and ends in "…".

The banner photograph shows another song lyric on its projector screen, behind the menu, as on the home page.

### V7 — Sermon page

`screenshots/sermon-desktop.jpg`, `p2-sermon-desktop-cookies-accepted.jpg`, `p2-sermon-mobile-scroll.jpg`.

The featured image (a YouTube thumbnail with black letterbox bars) is followed immediately by the video showing the same thumbnail, at a different width and indented differently, so the two do not line up. A circular date badge overlaps the first image's corner. With cookies refused, a blue button sits across the second thumbnail. The sidebar thumbnails are cropped mid-text ("BANDONNE / UR AVANC").

The article sits in a white card on a white page, marked only by a faint shadow. The text under the video is grey 16 px with no heading.

### V12 — Line length

Comfortable reading is 50 to 75 characters per line. Measured on a computer at 1440 px:

| Page | Median characters per line | Longest |
|------|---------------------------|---------|
| Faire un don (centred) | 157 | 157 |
| Génération Kids | 129 | 165 |
| Le couple pastoral | 101 | 113 |
| Conditions générales | 96 | 104 |
| Entraide | 93 | 125 |
| Notre histoire (two columns) | 74 | 82 |

Only the page laid out in two columns is in range. On Génération Kids the text runs the full 1,220 px of the content area (`p2-generation-kids-desktop.jpg`).

On a phone the opposite happens on some pages: Génération Kids has 85 px of margin on each side, leaving a 220 px column of about 28 characters (`p2-generation-kids-mobile-scroll.jpg`), while "Entraide" uses 280 px.

Centred paragraphs are used for running text on the home page, the donation page and "Entraide".

### V13 — Hover and focus states

- Links in the footer and the drop-down menu turn from dark (`#1e2f3c`, 13.8:1) to yellow (`#ffbd11`, 1.7:1) on hover: pointing at a link makes it fade.
- Keyboard focus removes the outline on every link and uses the same yellow, so focus and "current page" look the same and both are faint (`p2-desktop-menu-keyboard-focus.jpg`).
- Form fields get a 2 px yellow border on focus: visible on a phone capture (`p2-contact-mobile-field-focus.jpg`) thanks to its thickness, but at 1.7:1 against the beige field.
- Buttons barely change on hover (V3). Sermon cards have a hover overlay that was not captured.
- Text links inside paragraphs are dark blue with no underline (`#3a64aa` next to grey `#5b6b6f` text): colour alone marks them, and the two colours are close in lightness. The "ici" of the donation page is one of them.

### V14 — Date badges

Sermon cards carry a round badge with day and month, half over the image. Two colours alternate, blue and yellow, with no meaning (it follows the card's position). White on the yellow disc is 1.8:1. On the home carousel the badge is a small blue rectangle instead of a disc. No badge shows the year (UX review, U5).

### V15 — Cards and images

- Home carousel: cards have different heights because some thumbnails carry black letterbox bars and others do not (`p2-home-desktop-carousel.jpg`). The arrows are half outside the screen.
- Sermon thumbnails are designed images with their own typography, different every week; they are the liveliest visual material on the site, and the grid treats them well when they share one ratio.
- Department list: images are logos of very different styles, shapes and sizes, some on a grey square, some on white, one a photograph of the building (Mission). On a computer the rows alternate image left and right; on a phone the "F'Âmes Actuelles" logo is cut by the right edge of the screen (`p2-departments-mobile-scroll.jpg`).
- The "Entraide" logo is a low-resolution picture with a visible frame, enlarged.
- Three department logos are 1 to 3 MB files shown at 250 px (UX review, U11).
- The couple pastoral photo has its caption in a blue band inside the picture; good idea, used nowhere else.
- Génération Kids opens with three small photographs in a row, each cropped differently; on a phone only one shows before the text.
- Search results and related-sermon cards mix cards with and without images.

### V8 — Footer

`screenshots/home-desktop-footer.jpg`.

- The Facebook icon does not render: the word "FACEBOOK" appears in its place (its alt text), above full-colour Instagram and YouTube logos of different sizes. Confirmed on all 35 pages at both sizes.
- The logo block, two link columns and social icons are not aligned on a common grid; the left half of the footer is mostly empty on a computer.
- Footer links are 12 px bold on a phone, 15 px high, 24 px apart.
- The address and e-mail are set in Montserrat while the same facts are in Lato elsewhere.
- "© 2020 – Eglise évanglique de Valenciennes" (date and typo, see content review).
- The footer has no background or rule: it starts wherever the content stops, which on short pages (events, "Femmes", "Mission") makes the page look unfinished.

### V9 — Polish

- The accordion on the donation page uses the sky blue at full width with a heavy bar; the open panel holds one short sentence in grey 16 px, lighter than everything around it.
- A cookie-settings tab stays visible at the bottom right of every page.
- The contact page shows icons next to "Adresse", "Horaires", "Email", "Téléphones" in the logo blue, thin-lined, while the footer uses none.
- Pagination: the current page is a yellow disc, the others small bare numbers.
- The newsletter page is a bare form in the top-left corner of an empty page (`p2-newsletter-desktop.jpg`).
- A leftover theme page is public, in the theme's original black footer with unreadable dark-on-black links (`p2-theme-demo-page-desktop.jpg`).

### V16 — Phone details

- Page titles in the banner wrap to three lines for sermons and push the content below the first screen.
- Each page repeats its title as a second large heading right under the banner.
- The white card around forms and sermon articles leaves 40 px of margin, then 30 px of padding: the field width is 250 px on a 390 px screen.
- The date hint in the interview form is cut ("15/04/20…").
- On the home page the address panel loses its photograph and becomes a plain blue block; the departments band crops its photo to legs and feet.
- Nested bullet lists drop to 9 px.

### V17 — Tablet

`p2-home-tablet-768.jpg`. At 768 px the computer layout is kept and squeezed: one menu word and a yellow disc in the header, the headline against the left edge, the four buttons fused into one bar, three sermon cards with titles broken mid-word, and the address panel's heading broken as "Où sommes- / nous ?".

## Assets worth keeping

- The logo, in its colour and white versions.
- The real photographs of the congregation and building.
- The weekly sermon thumbnails, as a living visual stream.
- The department identities (Génération Kids, Punch Group) as illustrations, if they are still current and can be had as light files.
- Montserrat and Lato are a workable pair if weights are used with restraint.
- The warm beige of the form fields, as a starting point for a neutral that is not grey.
- The large form fields.

## For the redesign

1. A short scale: one body size of at least 16 px on every device, three or four heading sizes, two weights.
2. One blue for actions, one for surfaces, each checked against the text it carries.
3. One button, in two or three declared variants, 44 px high or more.
4. No text on a photograph without a backing; no meaning carried by yellow.
5. A reading column of 60 to 75 characters on a computer, with consistent gutters on a phone.
6. Image slots with fixed ratios, and files sized for their slot.
7. Visible hover and focus states that add contrast.

## Note on the child theme

`nazareth-child/functions.php` says "no CSS overrides exist in the child theme" and enqueues only the parent stylesheet, yet `nazareth-child/style.css` holds 228 lines of overrides. Either the parent theme loads the child stylesheet by itself or those overrides are dead. Production serves merged, minified CSS, so this could not be settled from outside; check on the local copy before relying on that file as a record of the current design.

One clue from the second pass: the orange `#f26837` defined in the child theme for the phone menu's active entry is applied on the live site, so at least part of that file is in use.
